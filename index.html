About Ron Mulder

Built From
the Ground Up

Ron Mulder is the founder of Redlum International — a consultancy born from three decades of hands-on experience building companies from inception to successful exit. His philosophy is simple: the end game should be part of the opening strategy.

Having operated across competitive sectors including aviation, government supply, calibration and test & measurement, Ron brings a rare combination of operational depth and high-level strategic thinking. He has navigated the full lifecycle of business — from early-stage formation and capital structuring to growth acceleration and ultimate divestiture — multiple times over.

Redlum International works with founders, boards, and executive teams who want to do things properly: build with purpose, scale with discipline, and exit with maximum value. Whether you are just starting out or preparing for a significant transaction, Ron provides the counsel that only lived experience can offer.

Where an engagement calls for it, Ron draws on a trusted network of seasoned business partners — specialists in their own right — to ensure clients receive precisely the right expertise at every stage of their journey. The result is boutique personal service backed by the collective depth of a much larger practice.
